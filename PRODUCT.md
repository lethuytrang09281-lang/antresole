# Antresole

## Product

- Platform: web.
- Product: portfolio website for Antresole, a design and architecture studio.
- Primary audience: prospective private clients evaluating the studio's spatial taste, rigor, and ability to carry an interior from concept through documentation.
- Primary job: let a visitor understand the studio through finished project material, then explore a project in depth.
- First proof surface: the residential interior project «Балтийский 99», supported by visualizations, a final plan, and a wall elevation.

## Confirmed assets and facts

- Brand mark: supplied raster logo «Antresole — design & architecture studio».
- The «Балтийский 99» project is documented as a 99 m² design project dated 2022.
- Available project material includes interior visualizations, a final plan, and wall elevations.
- Source files remain untouched; the website uses prepared derivatives only.

## Brand commitments

- The work leads; interface chrome stays quiet.
- Architectural-editorial composition with Swiss-modernist discipline: asymmetric grid, large imagery, precise typography, thin rules, and generous white space.
- Monochrome foundation; material color comes from the projects themselves.
- Plans and elevations are evidence, not decorative background.
- Motion is restrained and spatial: one deliberate reveal/transition system, never scattered effects.
- No SaaS cards, glass effects, gradients, ornamental shadows, or soft rounded containers.

## Content and trust constraints

- Do not present visualizations as built photography.
- Do not invent awards, press, services, locations, testimonials, metrics, contacts, or project outcomes.
- Do not publish personal phone numbers or title-block contact details from technical drawings.
- The first prototype may omit sections whose facts are not yet confirmed.

## First release

- A responsive portfolio entry point and a full «Балтийский 99» case experience.
- Russian editorial copy based only on the supplied material.
- Desktop and mobile layouts.
- Static-first implementation on the Sites/vinext React starter.

## Open decisions

- Public studio biography and team attribution.
- Public contact channels.
- Whether the project was realized or remains a design/visualization package.
- Final list and naming of services.
- Which additional projects enter the public portfolio.
